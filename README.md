# AI Engineer Academy — QA-to-AI Engineer Certification & Pro Hub Portal

An interactive, production-grade **AI Engineering Learning, Certification & Pro Hub Portal** built specifically for QA / Software Testing professionals transitioning into **AI Engineer** roles.

## ✨ Key Capabilities & Modules

1. **5 Core Learning Tracks (QA-to-AI Bridge)**
   - **Track 1**: AI & LLM Fundamentals (Tokenization, Embeddings, Self-Attention, Temperature vs. Top-P, Deterministic QA vs. Probabilistic Evals)
   - **Track 2**: Prompt Engineering & Anthropic Claude Mastery (XML System Prompts, Chain-of-Thought, Tool-Forcing, MCP, Side-by-Side Prompt Diff Sandbox)
   - **Track 3**: Google Cloud AI & Gemini Ecosystem (Vertex AI vs. AI Studio, Native Multimodal Audio/Video/PDF, Controlled Generation `responseSchema`, Search Grounding)
   - **Track 4**: Applied AI Engineering (Hybrid RAG with Dense Vectors + BM25 + RRF, Cross-Encoder Reranking, ReAct Agents, LLM-as-a-Judge with Ragas & DeepEval, AI Red Teaming)
   - **Track 5**: The AI Pulse (Reasoning Models & Test-Time Compute, Agentic Coding, Multimodal Live APIs, SLMs & Distillation, AI Governance & Official Certification Paths)
2. **3-Mode Concept Switcher on Every Lesson**
   - *Mode 1: Explain Simply*
   - *Mode 2: Explain with a QA/Testing Analogy*
   - *Mode 3: Technical Deep-Dive (with Production Code)*
3. **Interactive Animated Architectural Flowcharts & Active Recall Flashcards**
4. **Appreciation, Encouragement & Smart Notification Engine**
   - Dynamic time-of-day greeting header, `canvas-confetti` celebration bursts, Milestone Badges & Love Notes Wall, Web Notifications API Daily Study Nudges, and >24h Inactivity "We Missed You!" 2-minute refresher modal.
5. **Full Mock Certification Exam Engine (`/certification`)**
   - 40 scenario-based questions with a 45-minute timer, 5-Domain Diagnostic Skill Radar, and printable **Certified AI Engineer Readiness Certificate**.
6. **Pro AI Engineer Hub (4 Add-On Modules)**
   - **`/interview-prep`**: 26 scenario questions across 4 tabs with practice editor & Hiring Manager Rubrics.
   - **`/jargon-dictionary`**: 16 essential terms with Plain English, QA Analogy, and Production Code views.
   - **`/reference-docs`**: 13 official first-party guides across Anthropic, Google Cloud/Gemini, OpenAI, OWASP, Ragas & DeepEval.
   - **`/practical-labs`**: 6 progressive hands-on engineering challenges with a built-in **100-Point 4-Dimension Automated Rubric Grader** and Live LLM Judge Meta-Prompt exporter.
